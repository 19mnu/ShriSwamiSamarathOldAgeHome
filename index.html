About the care home

More than a place to stay. A place to belong.

Shri Swami Samarth Aaji-Aajobanchya care home aims to offer older adults a welcoming space where compassion, safety and human connection come first. Contact the team to learn about the home's current facilities, care arrangements and admission process.

Our purpose

To help senior citizens feel valued and supported through kindness, companionship and a respectful community.
